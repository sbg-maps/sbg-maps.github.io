SBG Maps Mod

Map editor

Custom maps for Super Battle Golf

Play maps made in the map editor with your friends. Maps the host has download to everyone in the lobby by themselves.

Download

Install

  1. In Steam: right-click Super Battle Golf, then Manage, then Browse local files.
  2. Extract the zip into that folder, so winhttp.dll and the BepInEx and SbgMaps folders sit next to Super Battle Golf.exe.
  3. Linux or Steam Deck only: set the launch options to WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%

Updating: extract the new zip over the old one. Everyone in a lobby needs the same version.

Play

  • Put .sbgmap files in Super Battle Golf/SbgMaps/maps/. They show up on the title screen, no restart needed.
  • Host a match and pick the Custom Maps course tab, or click Test a map on the title screen.
  • F5 restarts the hole (and re-reads the map file), F7 skips to the next one.

What maps can have

  • Imported levels or sculpted islands
  • Several holes per map
  • Surfaces: fairway, rough, sand, ice, climbable walls
  • Water level and out of bounds
  • Item boxes with chosen items
  • Red boxes
  • Jump pads
  • Golf carts
  • Checkpoints
  • Moving objects on paths
  • Music
  • Skies
